# Sample Exam Questions

## Sample Exam Question 1a

````{exercise} 
:label: exam_torsion_1a

Consider a thin-walled wing box structure of length $L = 4~m$ comprised of a semi-circular leading edge of radius $r = 50~mm$ joined to square of width $100~mm$ as illustrated below. The wing box is made of aluminum ($G = 25~GPa$) with a constant thickness, $t = 2~mm$, and is subjected to a uniform distributed torque of $500~Nm/m$. Answer the following questions related to this structure.

```{figure} ./Figures/exam_torsion_1a.svg
:alt: Closed wingbox profile with a uniform distributed torque
:width: 70%
:align: center
```
````

1. Draw the internal torque diagram for the wing box in the space provided below, being sure to clearly indicate magnitudes and signs of the internal torque for the given coordinate system.

2. Calculate the angle of twist of the wing box ($\theta_B$) with respect to the fixed end $A$ **AND** the magnitude of the maximum shear stress in the wing box due to the uniform distributed torque. The wing box is made of aluminum with $G = 25~GPa$.


````{solution} exam_torsion_1a
:class: dropdown

::::{tab-set}
:::{tab-item} Final Answer

1. Torque varies linearly from $-2~kN/m$ at $z = 0$ down to $0~kN/m$ at $z = 4~m$

2. $\theta_{B/A} = -2.71^\circ$, and $|\tau_{max}| = 36.0~MPa$


:::

::::
````


## Sample Exam Question 1b

````{exercise} 
:label: exam_torsion_1b

This is a followup question building off of the previous question:

A design modification to the wing box results in a $0.2~m$ length of the upper skin being removed between points $C$ and $D$ as illustrated below.

```{figure} ./Figures/exam_torsion_1b.svg
:alt: Wingbox subjected to a uniform distributed torque that is closed from the root along the majority of its span with the last 0.2 m having a cutout which makes the section open.
:width: 100%
:align: center
```
````

Calculate the new angle of twist of the modified wing box ($\theta_B$) with respect to the fixed end $A$ **AND** the magnitude of the maximum shear stress in the modified wing box due to the uniform distributed torque. The wing box is still made of aluminum with $G = 25~GPa$.


````{solution} exam_torsion_1b
:class: dropdown

::::{tab-set}
:::{tab-item} Final Answer

$\theta_{B/A} = -26.8^\circ$, and $|\tau_{max}| = 210~MPa$


:::

::::
````
