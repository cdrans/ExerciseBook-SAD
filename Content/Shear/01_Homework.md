## Exercise 3 (17.5)

````{exercise} 
:label: hw_shear_3

Consider the symmetric thin-walled channel profile shown below. The flanges each have a length $a$ and the web has a height $h$. The thickness of the flanges vary linearly from $t_1$ at their free ends to $t_2$ at their intersection with the web, while the web has a thickness of $t_3$. Derive the expression for the shear centre relative to the centre of the web ($\xi$ in the figure below).

```{figure} ./Figures/hw_shear_3.svg
:alt: Thin-walled symmetric channel profile with a constant thickness web and linearly variying thickness flanges. The web has a height $h$ and thickness $t_3$ while the flanges have a length $a$ and vary in thickness from $t_1$ at their free ends to $t_2$ at their intersection with the web.
:width: 60%
:align: center
```
````

````{solution} hw_shear_3
:class: dropdown
::::{tab-set}
:::{tab-item} Final Answer


$\xi  = \frac{{{a^2}\left( {2{t_1} + {t_2}} \right)}}{{3a\left( {{t_1} + {t_2}} \right) + h{t_3}}}$


:::
:::{tab-item} Intermediate Answers

Relevant Section Properties:

${I_{xx}} = \frac{{{h^2}}}{{12}}\left( {{t_3}h + 3\left( {{t_1} + {t_2}} \right)a} \right)$
:::
::::
````


## Exercise 4 (17.7)

````{exercise} 
:label: hw_shear_4

Consider the non-symmetric thin-walled channel profile shown below. Derive the expressions for the vertical and horizontal positions of shear centre relative to the intersection of the lower flange and web ($\xi$ and $\eta$ in the figure below).

```{figure} ./Figures/hw_shear_4.svg
:alt: Thin-walled symmetric channel profile with a constant thickness web and linearly variying thickness flanges. The web has a height $h$ and thickness $t_3$ while the flanges have a length $a$ and vary in thickness from $t_1$ at their free ends to $t_2$ at their intersection with the web.
:width: 60%
:align: center
```
````

````{solution} hw_shear_4
:class: dropdown
::::{tab-set}
:::{tab-item} Final Answer

$\xi  = \frac{{45}}{{97}}a$

$\eta  = \frac{{46}}{{97}}a$



:::
:::{tab-item} Intermediate Answers

Relevant Section Properties for coordinate frame $x$ posittive to the left and $y$ positive down:

${I_{xx}} = \frac{{16}}{3}{a^3}t$

${I_{yy}} = \frac{{53}}{{24}}{a^3}t$

${I_{xy}} =  - {a^3}t$
:::
::::
````
